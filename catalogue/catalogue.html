Welcome

Welcome to Zauq

Thank you for taking the time to explore our collection.

Zauq began with a simple belief: the walls around us should hold things that mean something. A verse that steadies us, a landscape that calms us, a photograph of the people we built something with. We create curated wall art and custom frames that bring that meaning into homes, offices and the spaces where people gather.

In this catalogue you will find our three collections, our custom and branded framing service, and everything a business needs to place an order — sizes, prices, delivery and how we work. If something catches your eye, or you have an idea of your own, we would love to hear from you.

About us

Art with taste,
framed by hand

Zauq Premium Frames is a framing studio in Rawalpindi creating curated wall art and custom frames for homes and businesses.

Our collection brings together Islamic calligraphy, modern and minimal prints and contemporary art. Alongside it, we print and frame our clients’ own photographs, artwork and logos to exactly the same standard.

Every piece is made to order. We print in high quality, set each artwork in an elegant black frame and finish it by hand, so it arrives ready to hang. A single statement piece and a set for an entire office receive the same attention.

We work with individuals and with organisations — offices, banks, hotels, cafés, clinics and showrooms — who want their spaces to feel considered and their gifts to feel personal.

Zauq
Urdu · noun

What our name means

Zauq is the Urdu word for taste — a refined sense of what is beautiful. It is the standard we hold every frame to: artwork chosen with care, proportions that suit the space, and a finish we are proud to put our name on.

Quality

Quality & craftsmanship

A frame is only as good as its details. Every Zauq piece is produced to order and leaves the studio finished, protected and ready for the wall — whether it is one frame for a reception or fifty for a gifting programme.

The frame

A premium black frame with a clean, modern profile. It lets the artwork lead and suits any interior, from a boardroom to a café.

The print

High-quality printing with rich colour and crisp detail. Every file is checked before printing, and we contact you if anything needs attention.

The finish

Each frame is cut for its artwork and finished by hand in Rawalpindi. Hanging hardware is pre-installed, so every piece is ready to hang.

Packing & delivery

Frames are packed securely to travel safely across the country, and delivered to any city in Pakistan — to one office or to many.

Our promise — if a frame arrives damaged or different from your order, we will put it right.

Frequently asked questions
Can we order in bulk for our company?
Yes. Tell us how many pieces you need, the sizes and your deadline, and we will quote for the whole order.
How long does an order take?
Made-to-order frames take approximately 7–12 working days. For larger orders, the timeline is confirmed in your quotation.
Which sizes are available?
Five standard sizes from 8 × 10 to 24 × 36 in. Collection pieces are shown at 18 × 24 in; other sizes on request.
Can you frame our logo or photographs?
Yes. Custom and branded frames are made from your files — JPG or PNG up to 20 MB — and every file is checked before printing.
Can we mix pieces from different collections?
Yes. Many spaces work best with a considered mix, and we are happy to suggest combinations.
Do you deliver outside Rawalpindi?
Yes — anywhere in Pakistan, to one address or to several.
Do the frames arrive ready to hang?
Yes. Hanging hardware is pre-installed on every frame.
Are corporate prices different from retail?
Retail prices are on page 10. Volume and corporate orders are quoted per project.
